Privacy Policy for Honey Barrel Comparator

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

Honey Barrel Comparator is a Chrome Extension that helps users compare prices of spirits on various retail websites with listings available on the BAXUS marketplace. We value your privacy and strive to be transparent about how your information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal or identifiable information from users.

The extension processes:

  • The URL of the currently active tab (when you click the "Find on Baxus" button).
  • Information scraped from the webpage, such as product name, price, ABV, and size — for the purpose of comparing it to BAXUS listings.

This data is not stored, tracked, or transmitted to any third-party service beyond the comparison service you explicitly invoke by clicking the button.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information from the page is temporarily used only to:

  • Extract bottle details.
  • Match those details against BAXUS marketplace listings via a backend API
  • Display matching results inside the extension popup.

No data is stored beyond the active session or sent to any external analytics or advertising platforms.

3. Permissions

This extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab: To access the URL of the tab you are viewing, only when you interact with the extension.
  • <all_urls> (host permissions): Required to extract content from e-commerce sites for bottle comparison.

These permissions are used solely to enable bottle data extraction and comparison. The extension does not track your browsing history or access any other tabs or sites without your direct interaction.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party.

All processing happens either:

  • Locally in your browser, or
  • Via a secure connection to your our backend API.

We use Firecrawl API, a third-party service, to extract data from websites. Firecrawl has its own privacy policy and data handling practices, which govern how they process and store data. By using this extension, you acknowledge and agree to Firecrawl's privacy practices.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to ensure your information is processed securely:

  • All communication with backend services is made over secure channels.
  • No personal or persistent data is stored in the extension or remotely.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to our practices. If updates occur, the new policy will be published with an updated effective date.
